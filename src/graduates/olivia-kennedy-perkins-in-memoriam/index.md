It is with profound sadness that we acknowledge the passing of Olivia on 25 October 2026, aged 22. An adored and deeply loved daughter of Roma and Angus, an awesome sister to Saint, and a much-loved granddaughter, niece and cousin. Olivia was also a treasured friend and valued member of our Year 3 Communication Design cohort. 

A beautiful, sparkling and vibrant soul, Olivia touched the lives of so many. She brought her own special energy and light to those around her. Dunedin and design held a special place in Olivia’s heart, and she will always hold a special place in ours.
