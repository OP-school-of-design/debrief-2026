projectName: Wild Smoke
projectDescription: "Wild Smoke is a portable fish smoker primarily designed for
  foot-access anglers who want to enjoy freshly smoked fish during their fishing
  trip, rather than waiting until they get home. The design addresses the bulk
  and inconvenience of traditional fish smokers with its compact tubular
  stainless-steel body, which allows all components to be packed neatly together
  for rattle-free transport. The smoker uses a methylated spirits burner as its
  heat source or alternatively can be put straight over a campfire. The project
  explores how a large cooking item can be redesigned for practical use in the
  backcountry country. "
