projectDescription: "Dogman Star is a vertical slice of a much larger 2D
  narrative side-scrolling puzzle game concept. It represents the split between
  a young person’s desire to follow their interests and their need for
  stability. The latter often leads to stable, decent paying jobs but
  consequently leaves you unsatisfied. It’s a feeling I understand and I want to
  use this game to say that suppressing your desires isn’t a sustainable
  lifestyle. The major challenge was that I had to build the game from the
  ground up in Unity, but the result is an experience that is all me, and I
  think that’s what I’m most proud of. "

Communicating feelings and narrative is what design is all about. That’s what is interesting about game design - you have complete control of the field. It’s overwhelming at first because there are so many moving parts, all of which are easy to break. But once you get the hang of it, you have access to the most immersive form of expression on Earth.
