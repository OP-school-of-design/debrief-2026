projectDescription: "This collection shows the internal realities of being
  neurodiverse juxtaposed with outward appearances, highlighting the differences
  between how people experience the world versus how they’re perceived. Each
  garment represents a different condition, personified as a fairy, with each
  design taking into consideration the different sensory needs of each
  condition. The primary inspiration for this collection comes from my own lived
  experiences, the experiences of people close to me, and the struggles of being
  frequently misunderstood, overlooked, or judged based on outward behaviour by
  other people. I’m challenging these misconceptions by encouraging audiences to
  look beyond appearances and consider the internal experiences that aren’t
  always visible. "

I am a storyteller. I am an artist. Fashion is my medium. Coming back to New Zealand I have tapped into the whimsy and inner child that I left behind. While training in Germany I had the opportunity to collaborate with GORE-TEX and the manufactures of Jablonec glass beads. In the future I hope to work between the two cultures.
