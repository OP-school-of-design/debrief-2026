projectDescription: "For my final project, I collaborated with VOID, a local
  Dunedin clothing retailer specialising in new and vintage pieces, to develop
  ABYSS, a mystery-driven marketing campaign. The project involved creating a
  distinct visual identity, promotional posters, social media content, and a
  website accessed through QR codes, with hidden clues building anticipation. A
  riddle box and giveaway introduced an interactive element, supported by two
  short promotional videos. The campaign came to life through a pop-up event
  featuring clothing displays, promotional posters, projected moving gradients,
  and atmospheric visuals. Combining grunge textures, experimental design and
  mystery, ABYSS x VOID explores new ways to engage audiences through an
  immersive retail experience. "
