As a designer, I am passionate about creating thoughtful, user focused products that improve people's experiences of life. Growing up around the hills of Wellington, I have always loved the outdoors and enjoy skiing, tennis and golf. Through hands-on design experience, I have developed an interest in sustainable practices, exploring ways to reduce waste, reuse materials and create products with a longer lifespan.
