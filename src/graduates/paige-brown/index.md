projectDescription: "“Spoiled” is a collection inspired by the 1966 film
  Daisies, by Věra Chytillová, which follows two young women who decide that if
  the world is spoiled, they will be too. The collection represents the
  transition from girlhood to womanhood, reminding wearers that freedom and
  creativity can remain as they grow, empowering a refusal to fit into a
  societal mould. Through bold colours and silhouettes inspired by Daisies and
  1960s cinema, the collection embodies playfulness and the desire to be
  noticed. Although rooted in girlhood, these garments aim to empower all
  identities to explore femininity and their own journey of being seen and
  heard. "

As a young female designer, my work is inspired by women past and present, using feminism and current affairs to inform my design process. I believe reflecting on the past helps improve the future. As a designer in Aotearoa, I value ethical and sustainable practices and hope to carry these principles into my future teaching career, inspiring the next generations of creatives.
