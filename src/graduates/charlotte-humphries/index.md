  I spent a season as a content intern with the Otago Rugby Football Union,
  capturing the grit, tradition and pride of southern rugby through film and
  photography. Working alongside the ORFU media team, I created match-day reels,
  player interviews and portraits for the NPC 50 Years campaign, Otago Spirit
  and Otago Community Rugby. 


  Blending heritage with modern short-form storytelling, the work reached audiences across Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, with one reel attracting over 68,000 views. It celebrates the players, volunteers and supporters who give Otago rugby its enduring identity. Visitors are invited to see Otago Rugby from the sideline.
