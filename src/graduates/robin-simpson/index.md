Hey there, I’m Robin - an illustrator and graphic designer based in Ōtepoti Dunedin. 
My approach to design is defined by merging digital and manual elements to create final outcomes that feel human-made and imperfectly perfect. This approach means that I’m always trying new techniques and design styles to see how I can creatively respond to briefs across print and digital media.
