Creativity has always been a big part of my life, from sewing at a young age to discovering unique finds while op shopping! I love experimenting with colour, fabrics, textures and embellishments to create designs that have a little bit extra. I enjoy adding decorative details that bring personality to my work, and when I’m not busy creating, you’ll usually find me spending time with animals!
