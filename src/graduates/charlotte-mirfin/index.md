  co-exist together. It features hidden details, imperfect flaws, and juxtaposed
  elements from dented metals and etched leather to soft cold draped fabrics.

  poetic through the contrast seen throughout; it reflects the confidence and
  vulnerability we internalise and express. Inspiration stems from the
  environment around me, the people, community, and the unpredictable challenges
  thrown at you by life.  I wanted to create a collection that questions the
  status quo of what beauty and perfection really mean and is untraditional in
  its ways of thinking and rebelling against these conventions. "

Be a boss, own it, and wear it with confidence. As an emerging designer, my design ethos is craftmanship, self-expression and authenticity. Exploring numerous textile developments has helped me gain a strong eye for detail while pushing the boundaries of what fashion can be. I specialise in leather developments, accessories and clothes, and plan to dive into fashion accessories in the future.
