I am Kashfia Asraf, an innovative visual merchandising and fashion styling professional who is currently studying at Otago Polytechnic. With extensive hands-on experience in retail management, my passion is designing retail spaces to incorporate fashion trend predictions using stories. My aim is to make fashion theories work in retail environments as well as integrating them with trends and public interest.
