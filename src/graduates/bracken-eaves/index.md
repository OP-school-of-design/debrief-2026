projectDescription: "Prom Night Never Ended is my graduate costume collection
  set in a fictional American high school in 1997, where prom night takes an
  unexpected turn with a zombie outbreak. Inspired by recognisable high-school
  stereotypes, I developed five characters: a jock, cheerleader, nerd, prom
  queen and school mascot. The collection follows their transformation six
  months after the outbreak, exploring how clothing that once represented school
  pride, identity and social status becomes evidence of survival. Through
  garment construction, textile experimentation and costume breakdown, I aimed
  to demonstrate how costumes can communicate character development and tell
  stories without the need for dialogue. "

I've always been fascinated by how clothing can communicate narratives. Throughout my fashion degree, I began to recognise how storytelling had become central to my design practice. This realisation sparked my passion for costume design, where clothing becomes a tool for developing characters and communicating their experiences. My subsequent experience working in the film industry further strengthened this creative direction.
