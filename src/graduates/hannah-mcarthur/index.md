projectName: Down the wrong rabbit hole
projectDescription: >-
  This collection was inspired by Lewis Carroll’s “Alice’s Adventures in
  Wonderland”, while also exploring the complexities of living with autism
  through familiar characters and the surreal world of Wonderland. Each
  look/character represents a different aspect of my autistic experience from
  masking and anxiety to emotional overwhelm and the challenges of
  communication. 



  I drew inspirations from each character using their personalities, stories and recognisable features to influence the silhouettes, colours and details throughout the collection. My aim was to challenge perceptions of what is considered normal and encourage a greater understanding of disabilities and disorders that are not always visible.

I've always been drawn to the expressiveness of textiles and how they make people feel, and a big focus of mine is to create ethical alternative fashion in New Zealand. I aim to create garments that not only make you feel and look good, but also feel considered in the way they are made and the message they carry.
