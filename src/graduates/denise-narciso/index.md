projectName: Sa Pagitan (In Between)
projectDescription: >-
  Sa Pagitan (In Between) is a practice-led design research project exploring
  migration, identity and belonging through the experiences of Filipino New
  Zealand creatives (Kiwinoys). Bringing together personal storytelling and
  kwentuhan (story sharing) with eight Kiwinoy creatives, the project culminated
  in Mga Kuwento sa Pagitan (Stories In Between), an immersive,
  multidisciplinary exhibition featuring vibrant, large-scale illustrations,
  moving image, sound, visual storytelling and a publication. 


  Extending beyond the exhibition, it also brought the community together through informal gatherings featuring karaoke, games and shared storytelling, fostering connection and cultural expression.

Kia ora! Mabuhay! I'm Denise Narciso, a Filipina illustrator, graphic designer and educator based in Aotearoa New Zealand.  I am a Principal Lecturer in Communication Design at Otago Polytechnic. My Master of Design project explores how lived experiences and cultural connections shape the way we create and understand the world.
