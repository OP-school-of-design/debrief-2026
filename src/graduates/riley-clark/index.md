From learning to read with graphic novels to my first success in theatre, visual communication has been a constant all throughout my life. Communication design is my obsession; a constant study that only expands. The more I learn about the power of visual storytelling, the more I believe in its potential to act as a catalyst in our pursuit of a better world.
