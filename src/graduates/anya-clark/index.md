I am inspired by hunting, motocross, rugby, and the outdoors, and aim to design products that help people do more of what they love without any barriers. My work uses human-centred design and systems thinking to create practical solutions that respond to real needs. I want to empower girls to get stuck in, take on challenges, and back themselves!
