projectName: Current
projectDescription: >-
  New Zealand’s beaches are a big part of our lifestyle, but keeping them safe
  comes with challenges. Current is a portable, handheld beach safety device
  designed to support lifeguards and improve beach safety for all New
  Zealanders.  


  It provides real-time beach data to help lifeguards quickly take preventive actions, while location-specific distress signals help reduce rescue response times. A help button and intercom also let the public communicate directly with lifeguards when they need help.  


  Current’s design focuses on being simple, durable, and practical, fitting into existing lifeguard routines without interfering with the experience of beachgoers.
