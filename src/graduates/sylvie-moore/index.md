projectDescription: "This project was inspired by traditional Scandinavian folk
  clothing and Midsummer traditions, integrated with my own appreciation for
  summer during my childhood in Nelson. I used influences from film and media,
  such as the 2019 film ‘Midsommer’, and the song ‘Suzanne’ by Leonard Cohen.
  During this project I only purchased one roll of fabric brand new, and the
  rest was upcycled table cloths and other various household linens, giving them
  a second lease of life. It was cool to see how each unique embroidered table
  cloth sewed up, and I am so happy with the final result!   "

Kia ora, I’m Sylvie! My inspiration stems from many things, including my friends and whānau, growing up as a competitive dancer, and my beautiful hometown upbringing in Nelson. However what has inspired me the most in this degree is having the ability to envision an item of clothing in my head, then translate it to a real garment.
