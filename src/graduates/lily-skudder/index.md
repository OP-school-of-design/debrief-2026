projectDescription: "Freestand is a redesigned walking stick that aims to make
  everyday life easier and more comfortable for young adults who need additional
  support. This project explores how thoughtful design can reduce the practical
  and social challenges associated with using walking sticks. Through hands-on
  research and prototyping, I developed features that allow the stick to rest
  securely against a table, stay within reach when not in use, and allow users
  to easily go hands-free if they need both hands. With a modern aesthetic and
  an option for personalisation, Freestand transcends the traditional medical
  appearance of mobility aids and helps users feel confident and independent in
  their everyday lives. "

I am passionate about creating thoughtful, inclusive designs that improve everyday life. My aim is to understand people’s needs and experiences, and then develop practical meaningful solutions that make products more accessible, functional and enjoyable to use.
