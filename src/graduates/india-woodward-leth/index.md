projectDescription: "What We Inherit is a celebration of childhood from the
  perspective of my mother as a girl in the 1970s. I started conversations about
  family history and expressed my whakapapa through fashion. In turn, coffee
  stains and cross-stitched borders show the whakapapa of the second-hand
  fabric. Once everyday necessities, skills like smocking and upcycling garments
  are being lost as we lose connection with our clothing. Conscious design
  choices allow these garments to be unpicked easily for ‘Design for
  Disassembly,’ so they can be reworked into new clothing. What We Inherit
  connects us to our planet, our family and our history. "

Exploring and manipulating fabric allows me to channel my creativity into tangible garments. My sustainable values are inspired by vintage clothing and nature, and by reworking second-hand fabrics and experimenting with unconventional alternatives, I make conscious efforts to minimise the use of plastic in design. My internship with Untouched World emboldened my ambition to work in a sustainable Aotearoa fashion brand.
