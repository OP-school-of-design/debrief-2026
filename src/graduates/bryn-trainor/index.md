  Freedom is something that sits at the core of who I am and I'm drawn to

  MORR SKATEBOARDS is a brand concept born from these values, bringing together my passions for skateboarding and design. This project allowed me to use skateboarding as a medium to apply my skills across different design disciplines. With freedom at the forefront, the project aims encourage the growth of skateboarding culture by bringing a fresh identity to Dunedin's skateboarding scene, all while still paying homage to its roots.

Kia ora! I’m Bryn - a designer born and raised in Dunedin who works across graphic design, illustration, and filmmaking. I believe creativity can be found everywhere, so I tend to draw inspiration for my work from my own experiences and scenes around me. Ultimately, I aim to create things that feel genuine and leave a lasting impression.
