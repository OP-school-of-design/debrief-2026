projectName: Hypnagogia
projectDescription: "Hypnagogia is a collection that explores the transitional
  state of consciousness between wakefulness and sleep. I wanted to take
  inspiration from the different ways our brain translates this state to us,
  visually, physically, and auditorily. This ready to wear collection features a
  mix of structured, sheer and knit fabrics featuring lino cut prints of imagery
  inspired by the 1920 silent film “The Cabinet of Dr. Caligari”, which I
  connected to my work through both the themes of subjective reality and bold,
  stylized shadows.  I hope there is something in this collection for everyone,
  and that the details and finishings can be appreciated for years to come. "
