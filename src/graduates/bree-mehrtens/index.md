projectDescription: "Tuku Iho is a story of growth and knowledge told through
  the Otago Polytechnic’s Cultural Narrative, designed with Te Punaka Ōwheo to
  gift to both prospective and graduate students. It includes a book, postcards
  and posters. Within the pages live whakataukī (Māori proverbs) passed down
  through generations, each one carrying wisdom, values, and unique ways of
  seeing the world. The book weaves photography, colour, illustration and text,
  to create meaning unique to each person who receives it. The style focuses on
  cyanotype photography of Otago Harbour, layered with illustrations of toroa,
  native plants, whakataukī and more. "

Creativity has always been a shining light in my life -  if I’m painting, reading or designing then I’m in my happy place. Design that shares this feeling is what inspires me. I want the people who encounter my work to feel something too; warmth, a little spark of inspiration, or that quiet recognition that something just feels right.
