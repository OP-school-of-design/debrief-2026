projectDescription: "This collection comes from a place of anger towards the
  historical oppression of women through marriage, and the traditions and
  expectations women have been forced to follow. Inspired by designers such as
  Juliette Hogan, Madeleine Vionnet and Dries Van Noten, as well as books about
  bridal history, the collection challenges traditional bridalwear. It responds
  to the lack of unique and unconventional bridalwear options in New Zealand,
  exploring wedding dresses that can be worn again rather than just on one day.
  Sustainability was also important, using existing fabrics from Juliette Hogan
  and finding ways to reduce waste throughout the design process.   "

As a designer, I like to experiment with drape to create silhouettes that accentuate the female body and curves. Experimenting with fabrics and textures to create unique garments. Breaking bridal traditions to create bridalwear that gives women freedom and evokes emotion. My internship with Juliette Hogan also influenced my project, as I learnt new skills and gained a better understanding of the fashion industry.
