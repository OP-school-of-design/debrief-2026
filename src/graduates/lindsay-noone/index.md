  design skills were developed to create static and video policy explainers,
  FAQs, and debate content that simplified their platform. For the campaign, I
  learned to direct, film, edit and add visual effects to create a video with
  grass-root marketing collaterals. Combined, these works increased candidate
  visibility, community engagement and student enrolments. "

Gidday! I’m a communication designer with a passion for photography, film, campaign creation and animation. Since high school, I’ve centred myself around translating complex stories into clear, concise and intuitive layouts. My mission is to simply solve problems through down-to-earth design solutions for brands and communities alike. In my down time, I produce music or work on the family farm.
