projectDescription: "Children deserve engaging and enriching learning
  experiences that help them build skills they will use for the rest of their
  lives. My goal with this project was to emphasise the role of play in early
  education and its benefits on young learners. Talk-Back provides children with
  a fun and hands-on way to develop their phonic skills. Using a combination of
  speakers and RFID (radio frequency identification) tags, young learners can
  interact with blocks to hear individual letter sounds, and experience how
  sounds blend to form words. Designed for multicultural Aotearoa, Talk-Back can
  be used to learn the foundations of any language with the push of a button. "
