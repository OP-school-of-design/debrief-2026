projectName: 133 kgs of Denim – Slow the Throw
projectDescription: >-
  I commenced my Master of Design project with the intention of creating a
  fibre-to-fibre system to recycle 133 kgs of denim I accumulated during the
  creation of my upcycled children’s wear label.  


  Subsequent research proved the premise valid; I uncovered many existing, yet unused, fibre-to-fibre solutions. My focus shifted to investigating systemic issues driving the non-implementation of these existing solutions.  


  I discovered that the outstanding driver of textile waste is overproduction and overconsumption. I designed ‘Slow the Throw’; a tool for consumers to become more aware and considered, enabling self-determination when purchasing, and slowing consumption through sustainable fashion shopping.

I graduated with a Diploma of Fashion and Design in 2000 and have spent many years in the fashion industry working as a designer, patternmaker, photographer and small business owner – all while being a mother of two. In recent years my focus has shifted to mitigating textile waste and promoting a more considered way of purchasing and loving our clothing.
