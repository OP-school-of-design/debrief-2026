projectDescription: >-
  For my DSM project I was an intern at Spellcaster, a game studio making their
  titular educational spelling game intended to appeal to older learners who
  struggle to connect with apps that are at their level but are targeted towards
  younger kids. 


  My task was to create artwork and animated visual effects (VFX) for some playing cards within a turn-based 1v1 minigame, in which a learner can cast a spell to damage their opponent, as long as they have correctly spelled a word given to them during their turn. I had a lot of fun getting creative with the designs!
