  into a valuable resource. Developed under the brand Scrappy Co., the primary
  outcome is a Kinusaiga craft kit that reimagines discarded textiles as a
  creative material. Designed with ImpacTex’s recycled textile panels, the kit
  can be returned for recycling at the end of its life, while its packaging
  serves a secondary function within the recovery system. Every component has
  been considered for reuse, recycling, or long-term value. As one touchpoint
  within a broader system, the project aims to raise awareness of textile waste
  and encourage sustainable practices. "

Growing up in a small mountain town in the Pacific Northwest, I developed a strong appreciation for the natural environment. This connection has helped shape my design philosophy, which is grounded in sustainability, circular thinking, and systems-led approaches. I enjoy exploring how design can create meaningful connections and encourage more thoughtful engagement with the world around us.
