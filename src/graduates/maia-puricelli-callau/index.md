projectDescription: "How to: Fold in ½  is an exploration of textiles in the
  context of a human body. The conceptual garments require human presence and
  interaction to situate them inside a fashion lens - only then the textile
  functions as a garment. The reacting and interreacting observed as the human
  body moves with textile – merging art, dance, and performance – informs my
  design process. While the collection is rooted in dynamic and transformable
  clothing, this project is intended to challenge what we label and understand
  as ‘garments’, and who has the agency to decide what is fashion.   "

My work in design and art is fuelled by the importance of challenging and redefining boundaries that confine expression, engagement, and playfulness. It is at the forefront of my practice to question and explore how a playful understanding of fashion can serve as a way forward for the industry, society, and the environment. This informs, directs, and redirects the ethos of my design journey.
