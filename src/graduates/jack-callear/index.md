projectDescription: "This project looks at how a catamaran-style foil board
  could improve stability and tracking without affecting performance. The idea
  came from how stable catamaran boats are, and I wanted to see if this design
  could make learning to foil easier for beginners while still being useful for
  more experienced riders on smaller boards. Through sketching, building and
  testing different prototypes, I explored different board shapes, sizes,
  volumes and foil sections to see what works best and understand the potential
  of this design. "

I’m a product designer from the north coast of Wellington, where growing up by the ocean has shaped my interests and projects. From surfboard bags to carbon foil boards, I enjoy creating products connected to the ocean. I’ve always been curious about how my favourite products are designed and manufactured, combining this curiosity with practical thinking to create products I’ll use long after graduating.
