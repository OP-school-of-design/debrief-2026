  translated through fashion, using tailoring and leather to interpret the seven
  deadly sins. The collection examines how pride, lust, greed, envy, and wrath
  influence the way individuals present themselves and interact with the world.
  Through exaggerated silhouettes, contrasting materials and manipulated
  construction, each look communicates a distinct psychological state.

  desire, control and excess. The collection uses fashion to communicate the
  complexities of human behaviour. "

My practice explores identity and transformation through conceptual fashion and experimental tailoring. Combining traditional tailoring techniques with unconventional silhouettes, leather and material contrasts, I use clothing as a medium to challenge conventions and explore the complexities of human experience.
