Crafting uniquely beautiful, sustainable designs that bring others joy is integral to my design identity. I was lucky enough to get an internship with Karen McDonnell the founder of Luma. Learning from her was incredibly insightful, especially since my long term goal is to start my own sustainable eveningwear brand. I enjoy exploring a combination of drape, zero-waste patterning, textile manipulations and bead embroidery.
