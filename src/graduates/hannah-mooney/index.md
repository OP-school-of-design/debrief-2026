projectDescription: "Whiskers Through the Wind is a narrative-focused, 2D
  side-scrolling game about developing empathy for animals without
  anthropomorphism. Set in the English countryside, the main focus is on illegal
  hunting traditions that are still being carried out, despite having laws in
  place that are meant to be protecting animals. The game’s protagonist is a
  young and clueless rabbit, on an adventure sparked by a fox being pursued by a
  hunter and his dog. She is guided through the farmlands by her ancestors, and
  finds friends in different animals who are also being persecuted and facing
  the impacts created by humans. "
