projectDescription: "Un-SCRATCHED reimagines the cassette for the streaming age.
  Music today is endless but weightless: rented, skipped and forgotten. This
  project brings back the ritual of choosing, holding and owning it. A companion
  app records digital tracks onto cassette, which plays through the tape deck of
  a home turntable or lifts out to go portable. Digital brings the convenience;
  the tape gives music somewhere to live. Grounded in research into the physical
  media revival and New Zealand copyright law, it explores how tangible
  listening rituals can return in a digital world, and why physical media and
  the stories they carry still matter.   "
