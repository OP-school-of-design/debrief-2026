projectDescription: >-
  KHARON is a series of independent zines with the purpose of commenting on the
  lack of critical thought amongst the general movie-going audience, and
  communicating how expanding their horizons into niche or cult film can help
  people appreciate the medium more. 


  It is a collection of 4 zines at this moment, with more to be developed over time. They are separated by genre and each contain articles and reviews of hand-picked films, but the introduction of more is always welcome.
