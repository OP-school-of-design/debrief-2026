projectName: Getting Dressed In The Dark
projectDescription: >-
  Would you be able to dress yourself in the dark? Vision is generally the focus
  when styling oneself - however this collection explores how the methodology of
  dressing changes when dressing in the dark. Without lights, you rely on other
  phenomenological senses to identify what garment you’re holding and if you’re
  wearing it correctly. 


  I drew on the methodologies of adaptive fashion, light and colour theory, along with research on low vision as my guiding framework, and designed a fully reversible size 6-18 collection using Gerber patternmaking software, projectors, and a laser cutter. The goal was to focus on strengthening and developing commercial industry skills.
