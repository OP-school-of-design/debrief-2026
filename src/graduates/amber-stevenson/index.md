projectName: Growing Up
projectDescription: >-
  ‘Growing Up’ follows three main stages in my life that have really helped me
  become the person I am today, and influenced who I could become tomorrow. The
  project represents how, without noticing, we tend to carry traits of our past
  selves through into new experiences and learnings, as we grow into our
  evolving identities. 


  I took each of the three main life stages and turned them into individual photographic narratives with appropriate garments and locations. Exploring fashion communication has allowed me to develop my creative interests and gain a clearer understanding of my future career aspirations.

Emotion and passion drive my design work. Any topic that makes me feel wholesome, angry or sad inspires me to really delve into it and create work informed by my research. My goal is to capture powerful stories and themes from my own fashion collections or those of others, and bring them alive through styling and photography.
