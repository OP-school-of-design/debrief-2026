projectName: Carousel Rebrand & Underground Sound
projectDescription: >-
  Carousel is a local bar that brings records and cocktail culture together
  under the same needle. While their ethos is well known, their branding had
  been an afterthought for far too long. The goal of this project was to bring
  this up to tempo and inject some new character into their brand story. A
  hand-crafted logo refined digitally sets the tone for a refreshed visual
  identity that more accurately reflects the duality and voice of the venue. 


  A launch event takes advantage of this new look and shines a spotlight on local music talent and some ‘Underground Sound’, with all ticket proceeds going to the Breast Cancer Foundation NZ.
