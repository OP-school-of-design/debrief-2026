Kia ora, I’m Caleb, a designer from Ōtepoti. My skills lie in graphic design, photography, film and illustration. While drawn to bold, grungy, handmade aesthetics, I adapt my creative approach to best uphold each project’s vision and values. Influenced by the DIY ethos of alternative music culture, I value authenticity, creative expression and social responsibility, aiming to create thoughtful, distinctive designs with a positive impact.
