DEBRIEF is the annual exhibition of final-year students from the School of Design at Otago Polytechnic. It showcases the creative talents and innovative projects of our graduating students across various design disciplines, including Communication Design, Fashion Design, Product Design, and Postgraduate Design. Each project reflects the students' dedication, creativity, and the skills they have honed during their time at Otago Polytechnic. We invite you to immerse yourself in the world of design and witness the future of creativity. Enjoy the exhibition and discover the next generation of design talent!
