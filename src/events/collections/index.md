Otago Polytechnic COLLECTIONS 2026 is a much-anticipated annual fashion show showcasing the work of Otago Polytechnic fashion ākonga.

We’d love you to join us for a celebration of fashion and creativity at our COLLECTIONS 2026 show where our graduating fashion students will be showcasing their latest collections, capturing the energy, originality, and vision of the next generation of New Zealand fashion designers.

The COLLECTIONS 2026 runway also features work from our Year 1 and 2 students, offering a rare glimpse into the full spectrum of fashion learning and development at Otago Polytechnic

*Doors open at 7.00pm for an 8pm start of show
Cash/Eftpos Bars available - ID will be required*
